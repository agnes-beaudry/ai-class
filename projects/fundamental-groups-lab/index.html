THE VAN KAMPEN RECIPE

From geometry to a presentation

1

Pick loops

After gluing, named edge pairs become loop generators based at the identified vertex.

2

Read the boundary

Walk around the polygon. A reversed arrow contributes an inverse, such as a⁻¹.

3

Attach the face

The filled polygon makes its boundary loop null-homotopic, so its word becomes a relation.

4

Simplify

Write generators and relations as ⟨ generators | relations ⟩ and recognize the group when possible.

Key idea. The drawing is a one-vertex CW model: loops are the 1-cells, while the relation comes from attaching a 2-cell.
