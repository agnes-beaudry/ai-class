Gemini · first response

The final step of the proof

Easy-to-check details leading up to this sentence — a vague statement that glosses over details.

Since C• possesses a homotopy commutative product
resolving the multiplication on R∨ (arising from the cup
product of the spherical cellular structure), the map u
satisfies u ∘ σ ≃ u in the derived category.
Consequently, v ≃ (−1)^d u.

Asymmetry of effort. That paragraph is the theorem, stated as a parenthetical.

Phase 2 · The worked case

At the end of the same response

A useful sanity check: d = 1 supports the sign.

    h( (a₊,a₋) ⊗ (b₊,b₋) ) = ā₊b₋ − ā₋b₊

Four lines of explicit formula. Only concrete object models had produced all day — not even an answer to my question.

Phase 2 · The prompt that changed the outcome
me → ChatGPT · line 3525 of 14,199

"You did such a good job with the d=1 case, I want an explicit proof like that which I can verify without doubt. If you don't see the general argument and you want to start by proving it for d=2 and d=3 first, we can look at those arguments together and see how they may generalize?"

Name what worked

Point at the concrete thing it did.

Direct to concrete examples

d = 2, then d = 3. Small enough that bluffing is impossible.

Getting started

ChatGPT or Claude, in ordinary web chat

Open with this:

I'm a mathematician working on the question below. Work with me as a
mathematical collaborator. Start with your assessment of the problem and
the most promising next step. If you see a short complete proof, give it.
Otherwise, develop the first substantive step and pause so that I can
respond. State precisely any lemma you are assuming.

Here is my question:  [question, definitions, hypotheses]
Here is what I have tried, and where I am stuck:  [your attempt]

Settings. ChatGPT: GPT-5.6 Sol, Medium reasoning. Claude: Fable 5.1 or Opus 5, High effort. Raise effort only once you've isolated a hard step. If a request runs several minutes with nothing usable, interrupt and ask something smaller.
