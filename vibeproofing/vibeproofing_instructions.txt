VIBEPROOFING: GETTING STARTED

Use ChatGPT or Claude in ordinary web chat. Bring a mathematical question
you care about, along with any relevant definitions or attempted argument.

OPENING PROMPT

I'm a mathematician working on the question below. Work with me as a
mathematical collaborator. Start with your assessment of the problem and
the most promising next step. If you see a short complete proof, give it.
Otherwise, develop the first substantive step and pause so that I can
respond. State precisely any lemma you are assuming.

Here is my question:
[Insert your question and relevant definitions or hypotheses.]

Here is what I have tried, and where I am stuck:
[Describe your attempt or the specific point you want help with.]

MODEL SETTINGS

- ChatGPT: Start with GPT-5.6 Sol at Medium reasoning, if available.
  Increase effort when you have isolated a difficult step.

- Claude: With Fable 5.1 or Opus 5, start with High effort, which Claude
  recommends as a balance of quality and speed. Click the model name
  beside Send, then Effort. Reserve Extra High or Max for a difficult
  step. Thinking cannot be switched off for these models in web chat.

Available models and controls depend on your account. These settings and
the prompt do not guarantee a response time. If a request takes several
minutes without a usable response, consider interrupting and asking a
smaller question.

Read and question the mathematics as it arrives. A complete solution is
welcome: understanding and checking it can be the next part of your work.
